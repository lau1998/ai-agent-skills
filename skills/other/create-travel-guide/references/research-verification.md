# Research And Verification

## Follow The Source Hierarchy

Prefer sources in this order:

1. Government, embassy, border authority, transport operator, venue, and service-provider official sources.
2. Official tourism, weather, road, emergency-management, and public-safety sources.
3. Reputable maps, ticketing, and booking platforms for route, schedule, availability, and price cross-checks.
4. Reputable local media and established travel references for context and experience details.
5. Social media for inspiration only, never as the basis of a current fact.

Use one conclusive, current official source when it directly answers the question. For ordinary facts without a conclusive official answer, confirm the fact with at least two independent sources. Independence requires different publishers or primary data, not two pages repeating the same claim.

Treat entry, visa, legal, health, permit, and public-safety requirements as consequential. Leave each consequential fact unresolved unless the responsible government, consular, legal, health, permit, or public-safety authority, or an explicitly official equivalent, verifies it for the applicable traveler and date. Use secondary sources only to cross-check or locate the authority; two secondary sources never replace it.

## Scope Narrow Queries

For a single-fact query, skip the full trip intake but collect the minimum qualifiers needed for that fact before research. Ask for citizenship or travel-document issuing jurisdiction and type, document expiry, residence when relevant, travel purpose, and exact date for entry or visa questions; never request a passport or document number. Ask for exact date, endpoints, direction, traveler category, party size, and occupancy or fare conditions when relevant to transport, accommodation, or price. Ask no unrelated itinerary questions.

## Verify Against Exact Travel Dates

Check every material dynamic fact for the travel date, destination, traveler, and transport mode:

- Entry, visa, passport, insurance, health, and permit requirements.
- Weather, daylight, seasonal conditions, holidays, strikes, warnings, and public-safety notices.
- Opening hours, temporary closures, tickets, reservations, timed entry, and visitor restrictions.
- Flights, rail, ferries, transit, transfers, and driving feasibility.
- Road closures, tolls, parking, fuel, charging, daylight, mountain conditions, and driver fatigue.
- Age, health, equipment, operator, and legal restrictions for high-risk activities.
- Accommodation, transport, admission, and other prices that materially affect the budget.

Do not use a route, schedule, opening time, restriction, or price from a nearby date as if it applies to the trip date.

## Keep A Verification Record

Record each critical fact with:

| Field | Required content |
| --- | --- |
| Fact | The precise claim used in the guide |
| Applicable date | The date or validity period the claim covers |
| Source | Publisher, page title, and direct link |
| Verification date | The date the source was opened and checked |
| Confidence | High, medium, or unresolved, with a short reason |

For every price, also record currency, unit, inclusions, exclusions when material, and query time. Mark estimates explicitly and state the calculation or source basis. Never convert an unverified remembered price into an exact figure.

Carry source traceability into the final guide or a clearly linked appendix. Do not remove citations for presentation, client-facing use, brevity, or visual polish.

## Reject Weak Evidence

- Never cite a search result snippet without opening and checking the source page.
- Never use model memory as a current fact.
- Never treat one blog or social post as sufficient evidence for a current claim.
- Never copy a booking-platform result without checking its dates, occupancy, taxes, fees, cancellation terms, and query time when those affect the claim.

## Stop On Verification Failure

- **Network unavailable:** Stop final-guide production. State which current facts remain unverified and what can resume when access returns.
- **Official page unavailable or inconclusive:** For an ordinary fact, seek an official alternative or two independent reputable sources and disclose the limitation. For a consequential fact, seek another responsible authority or explicitly official equivalent; otherwise leave it unresolved and stop the affected advice.
- **Sources conflict:** Prefer the responsible authority and the source with the most current applicable date. If the conflict remains material, do not choose silently; mark it unresolved and exclude the affected plan.
- **Critical fact unverified:** Stop the affected itinerary segment. Offer only alternatives whose route, availability, restrictions, and dates have been verified.
- **No safe verified alternative:** Explain the gap and request a date, route, or activity change. Do not deliver a pseudo-complete itinerary with guessed details.
